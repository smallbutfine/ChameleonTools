MIDI GENERATION

ChameleonDrummer

A dynamic MIDI drum generator that turns a genre, style, drummer, and meter into a realistic, human-feeling drum track. It exports MIDI files that you can import into any DAW, drum software, or notation program.

Who it's for: Songwriters, producers, and composers who need realistic drum tracks without a drummer or a full drum kit. Also useful for generating ideas, sketching arrangements, and creating backing tracks.

What makes it different: It doesn't just loop samples â€” it models drummer styles (Bonham, Porcaro, Chambers, Roeder, Dee, Hoglan, and 10 more), applies humanization (timing jitter, ghost notes, swing), and respects physical playability constraints. The result sounds like a real drummer, not a machine.

  • 5 genres, 37 styles, 17 drummer profiles
  • Multi-keymap support (GM, AD2, EZDrummer 3, Yamaha XG, custom JSON)
  • Compound and odd meters (5/4, 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 13/8, up to 32/16)
  • Triplet feel, swing, and humanization
  • REAPER and Ardour session export
  • CLI for batch generation, GUI for interactive use

AUDIO CONVOLUTION & MASTERING

TaraDSP

A professional FFT-based convolution processor for cabinet simulation, convolution reverb, and mastering-grade audio processing. It applies impulse responses to audio signals with studio quality.

Who it's for: Guitarists and bassists who want cabinet simulation, audio engineers who need convolution reverb, and mastering engineers who need TPDF dithering, minimum-phase conversion, and high-quality resampling.

What makes it different: It uses the PFFFT library (Pretty Fast FFT) for real-FFT convolution â€” twice as fast as complex FFT for real audio signals. It includes mastering-grade TPDF dithering with error feedback, minimum-phase conversion (removes pre-ringing), and multi-engine resampling (SoX, R8Brain, FinalCD, linear fallback). It can also combine two IRs into a new one â€” blend two cabinets, bake EQ into a cab, or merge a cab with a room.

  • FFT convolution with PFFFT (SIMD-accelerated)
  • TPDF dithering with error feedback (16-bit)
  • Minimum-phase conversion (no pre-ringing)
  • Multi-engine resampling (SoX / R8Brain / FinalCD / linear)
  • IR combination (merge two impulse responses)
  • Fades, silence trimming, normalization, truncation
  • CLI for batch processing, GUI for interactive use

INSTRUMENT SETUP

StringSelectorApp

A string tension calculator for guitars and basses. It calculates the exact tension of every string based on gauge, unit weight, tuning, and scale length â€” and generates printable workshop reports.

Who it's for: Guitar techs, luthiers, bass players, and anyone who needs to choose the right strings for a tuning, set up a multiscale instrument, or check tension before a truss rod adjustment.

What makes it different: It uses unit weight (not just gauge) for accurate tension calculation â€” two .010" strings from different brands can have 20% different tension. It includes a visual tension curve, a truss rod adjustment assistant (estimated turns based on tension difference), and printable HTML workshop reports. It supports 4â€“9 string instruments and multiscale (fanned-fret) scale lengths.

  • 37 string packs (Ernie Ball, D'Addario, Elixir, and more)
  • 28 tuning presets (Standard, Drop, Open, custom)
  • Real-time visual tension curve
  • Truss rod adjustment assistant (estimated turns)
  • Printable HTML workshop reports
  • CSV export, custom tuning save, transpose/pitch
  • ISO units toggle (lbs / kg / N)
  • Multiscale support (treble + bass scale)

HEADLESS RECORDING

ChameleonRecorder

A headless, plug-and-play multitrack WAV recorder OS for the Raspberry Pi 4 (64-bit). It automatically detects any class-compliant USB audio interface, records uncompressed 24-bit multitrack WAV files directly to a USB flash drive, and provides a mobile-friendly web remote control with live level meters.

Who it's for: Live sound engineers, rehearsal studios, and musicians who need to record multitrack audio without a laptop. Also useful for installation recording, choir/orchestra capture, and any situation where a dedicated, silent, low-power recorder is preferred over a computer.

What makes it different: No laptop. No DAW. No drivers. Just a Raspberry Pi, a USB audio interface, and a USB stick. Power on and record. It includes an automatic fallback Wi-Fi hotspot (SSID: ChameleonRecorder) so it works even without a router â€” connect your phone and control everything from the browser. It supports multi-device recording (multiple USB interfaces simultaneously), ADAT clock sync for sample-accurate multi-unit setups, and optional GPIO pushbuttons for physical Record/Stop control.

  • Universal USB audio support (Behringer, MOTU, Focusrite, RME, Zoom, etc.)
  • Multi-device recording with automatic ADAT clock sync
  • Web remote on port :8080 (per-device & per-channel arming, live meters)
  • Fallback Wi-Fi hotspot (no router needed)
  • 24-bit/48kHz WAV recording directly to USB flash drive
  • GPIO pushbuttons + status LED (optional)
  • File management: delete, download, ZIP archive
  • SSH access for advanced users
